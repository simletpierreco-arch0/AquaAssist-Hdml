About NAWASA

Delivering safe, reliable water services across Grenada

NAWASA is responsible for the treatment, supply, and quality of drinking water, along with sewerage services, across Grenada, Carriacou, and Petite Martinique. Our teams work around the clock to keep service reliable and respond quickly when something goes wrong.
